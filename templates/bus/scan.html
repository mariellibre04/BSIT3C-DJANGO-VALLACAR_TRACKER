1. Choose terminal

Every scan records a terminal tap and updates the bus location on the live map.
Choose a terminal, then start the camera and point the bus QR at it.
Move the QR closer or farther until the modules look sharp (about 15–30 cm), keep it flat, and avoid glare. Camera access needs localhost or HTTPS. If it fails, allow the camera from the camera icon in the address bar and check Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Camera. Scans are recorded instantly, so you can scan the next bus right away.
